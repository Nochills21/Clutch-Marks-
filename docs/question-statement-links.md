## How the links were made

`node .freebuff/link-question-statements.cjs --apply` wrote them, in three tiers
of confidence:

1. **Single-statement topic** (766 questions) — the topic owns exactly one
   statement, so the mapping is definitional rather than inferred.
2. **Verbatim title** in the stem or explanation (≈215) — the question names the
   subsection it tests.
3. **TF-IDF cosine** against the topic's own statement titles (≈698), accepted
   only at a floor and only with a margin over the runner-up, and only when the
   winning token appears in the stem itself.

### Known limits, stated plainly

- **1,839 questions were left null on purpose.** 471 of them sit in topics with
  no statements at all (both English qualifications, plus gaps in Maths and
  Physics A2), where no link is possible. The rest had no unambiguous evidence:
  a wrong link inflates a subsection that does not contain the question, and
  `syllabus_coverage()` cannot tell the difference, so null was preferred.
- **Options are not evidence.** The first pass matched statement titles anywhere
  in the item and filed "Which technique separates ethanol from water?" under
  *Chromatography*, because chromatography was a distractor choice. The rule now
  reads the stem and the explanation only.
- **Legacy answer keys are not evidence either.** "Group 0 elements are called"
  carries `correct_option = 2`, which points at *Alkali metals* rather than
  *Noble gases* — a wrong key on a live question. Any repair or check that leans
  on `correct_option` in these banks is leaning on a field that is wrong in
  places.
- **Two hand-read samples put precision at about 20 of 22.** One sample of eight
  chemistry links contained the chromatography error (now fixed); a random sample
  of 14 across six subjects contained one more, a flux-density question filed
  under *Force on a current-carrying conductor* rather than the field-of-a-wire
  statement. Treat per-statement counts for the legacy banks as an estimate, not
  a certified map. The 9202 bank is the one whose links are authored rather than
  inferred.
- `node .freebuff/audit-statement-links.cjs` re-derives every link and lists the
  ones that no longer follow from the rules — 1,956 of 2,295 did after the
  backfill, and most of the remainder are *correct* links with no shared wording
  ("a 4 Ω and a 6 Ω resistor in series" under *Electric circuits*). It is a
  review list, not a verdict.
