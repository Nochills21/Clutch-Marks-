Circular Measure

Syllabus: Cambridge 9709 Pure Mathematics 1, section 1.4 Circular measure: radian measure, arc length and sector area.

1. Radians

One radian is the angle at the centre of a circle subtended by an arc whose length equals the radius. The full circumference is 2πr, so a full turn is 2π radians and π radians = 180°.

  • Degrees to radians: multiply by π/180. Example: 150° = 150π/180 = 5π/6 ≈ 2.618 rad.
  • Radians to degrees: multiply by 180/π. Example: 1.5 rad = 1.5 × 180/π ≈ 85.9°.

Learn the common values: 30° = π/6, 45° = π/4, 60° = π/3, 90° = π/2, 180° = π, 360° = 2π.

2. Arc length

For an angle θ in radians, the arc length is s = rθ.

Example: r = 6 cm, θ = π/3. Then s = 6 × π/3 = 2π ≈ 6.28 cm.

Perimeter of a sector: P = 2r + rθ (two radii plus the arc).

Example: r = 8 cm, θ = 0.7 rad. The arc is 5.6 cm, so P = 16 + 5.6 = 21.6 cm.

3. Sector area

A = ½r²θ.

Example: r = 10 cm, θ = π/3. A = ½ × 100 × π/3 = 50π/3 ≈ 52.4 cm².

Example: r = 5, θ = 1.2. A = ½ × 25 × 1.2 = 15.

4. Segment area

A segment is the region between an arc and its chord. The triangle formed by the two radii and the chord has area ½r² sinθ, so the segment area is A = ½r²(θ − sinθ).

Example: r = 2, θ = π/2. A = ½ × 4 × (π/2 − 1) = π − 2 ≈ 1.14.

Chord length: the chord is 2r sin(θ/2). For r = 4, θ = π/2 the chord is 8 sin(π/4) = 4√2 ≈ 5.66.

5. Working backwards

  • θ = s/r (θ must be in radians)
  • θ = 2A/r² (θ in radians)

Example: an arc of length 12 cm on a circle of radius 4 cm gives θ = 12/4 = 3 rad ≈ 171.9°.

6. Major and minor sectors

If the given angle θ belongs to the minor sector, the major sector has angle 2π − θ, so its area is ½r²(2π − θ).

Example: r = 3, θ = 1 rad. Major sector area = ½ × 9 × (2π − 1) = 9π − 4.5 ≈ 23.8.

Exam technique

  • Convert every angle to radians before using s = rθ or A = ½r²θ. Mixing degrees in gives wrong answers.
  • Put the calculator in radian mode for any trigonometry inside a circular-measure question.
  • Sketch the figure and mark r and θ first, and check whether the question asks for the minor or the major sector.
