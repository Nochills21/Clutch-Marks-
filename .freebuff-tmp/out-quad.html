Quadratics

Syllabus: Pearson Edexcel International AS Mathematics, Pure Mathematics 1 (WMA11), sections 1.2 to 1.9: surds, quadratic functions, the discriminant, completing the square, simultaneous equations and inequalities.

Section 1

1. Factorising and solving

Factorise by finding two numbers that multiply to the constant term and add to the coefficient of x. Set each bracket to zero to find the roots.

Example. x² + x − 6 = (x + 3)(x − 2), so x = −3 or x = 2.
Section 2

2. The quadratic formula

For ax² + bx + c = 0, the roots are x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) ÷ 2a.

Example. x² − x − 1 = 0 gives x = (1 ± √5) ÷ 2, so x ≈ 1.618 or x ≈ −0.618.
Section 3

3. The discriminant

The discriminant is b² − 4ac. It tells you the number of real roots:

  • b² − 4ac > 0: two distinct real roots.
  • b² − 4ac = 0: one repeated real root; the graph touches the x-axis.
  • b² − 4ac < 0: no real roots; the graph does not meet the x-axis.
Example. for 2x² − 4x + 5, the discriminant is 16 − 40 = −24, so there are no real roots.
Section 4

4. Completing the square

Write ax² + bx + c in the form a(x + p)² + q. The turning point is at x = −p, with value q. The sign of a shows whether it is a minimum (a > 0) or a maximum (a < 0).

Example. x² + 6x + 5 = (x + 3)² − 4. The minimum is at (−3, −4).
Section 5

5. Surds

To rationalise a denominator, multiply top and bottom by the conjugate. Example: 1 ÷ (3 + √2) = (3 − √2) ÷ 7.

Section 6

6. Simultaneous equations (line and curve)

Substitute the linear equation into the quadratic and solve the result.

Example. y = x + 1 and y = x² give x² − x − 1 = 0, the same quadratic as above. Substitute each root back to find its y-value.
Section 7

7. Quadratic inequalities

Sketch the graph and read off the region. For x² − 5x + 6 < 0, the roots are 2 and 3 and the parabola opens upwards, so the solution is 2 < x < 3.

Exam technique

Mark-winning habits

  • Show the discriminant calculation before stating the number of roots.
  • Check each root by substitution.
  • For inequalities, test a value inside the region to confirm the direction.
