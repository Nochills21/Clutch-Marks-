  0: 0.75
  1: 0.5

  3: 0.6   <-- KEY
EXP: Use the sum to infinity formula \(S_\infty = \frac{a}{1 - r}\) M1 [1]. Substitute \(a = 20\) and \(S_\infty = 50\): \(50 = \frac{20}{1 - r}\), so \(1 - r = 0.4\) and \(r = 0.6\) A1 [1].
